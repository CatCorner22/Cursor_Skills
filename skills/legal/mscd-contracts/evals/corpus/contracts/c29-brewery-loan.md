# Loan and Security Agreement

This loan and security agreement is dated May 3, 2027, and is between Summit Ledger Capital, LLC, a Colorado limited liability company ("**Lender**"), and Larkspur Hollow Brewing Co., a Colorado corporation ("**Borrower**").

The parties hereto agree as follows:

A. **Definitions.**  In this agreement:

1. "**Collateral**" shall mean all of Borrower's brewing equipment, fermentation tanks, canning line, and replacement parts, and all proceeds of that property.

2. "**Loan**" means the loan that Lender makes to Borrower under this agreement.

3. "**Maturity Date**" means May 3, 2032.

B. **Loan.**  Lender shall lend Borrower $850,000.00 on the date of this agreement.  Borrower shall use the Loan only to buy and install brewing equipment at its brewery at 4180 Wewatta Street, Denver, Colorado.

C. **Interest.**  Interest shall accrue on the unpaid principal of the Loan at a fixed rate of 7.25% per year.  Lender shall compute interest on the basis of a 360-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.  While an Event of Default continues, the interest rate is 4 percentage points higher than the fixed rate.

D. **Repayment.**  Borrower shall pay Lender monthly installments of principal and interest of $16,933 on the first day of each calendar month, starting June 1, 2027, and shall pay all unpaid principal and accrued interest on the Maturity Date.

E. **Prepayment.**  Borrower may prepay the Loan in whole or in part at any time, provided that Borrower gives Lender at least 10 days' notice and pays Lender a prepayment fee equal to 2% of the principal that Borrower prepays.

F. **Security Interest.**  The Loan shall be secured by a security interest in the Collateral.  Borrower authorizes Lender to file financing statements describing the Collateral, as Section 4-9-509 of the Colorado Revised Statutes permits.

G. **Statements of Fact.**  Borrower represents and warrants to Lender that:

1. Borrower is a corporation in good standing under Colorado law;

2. Borrower owns the Collateral free of any lien other than Lender's lien; and

3. Borrower has paid all taxes on the Collateral that are due.

H. **Covenants.**  Until Borrower has repaid the Loan in full:

1. Borrower shall keep the Collateral insured against loss for the full replacement value of the Collateral and shall name Lender as lender loss payee.

2. Borrower shall deliver its annual financial statements to Lender no later than 120 days after the end of each fiscal year.

3. Borrower shall not sell, lease, transfer, and/or encumber any of the Collateral without Lender's written consent.  Any transfer in violation of this subsection is null and void.

I. **Events of Default.**  Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

1. Any amount under this agreement remains unpaid thirty (30) days after its due date.

2. Borrower breaches any covenant in section H and does not cure the breach no later than 15 days after Borrower receives notice of the breach from Lender.

3. Borrower becomes the subject of a case under the U.S. Bankruptcy Code.

J. **Remedies.**  If an Event of Default occurs, Lender may declare the Loan immediately due and may exercise the rights of a secured party under Article 9 of the Colorado Uniform Commercial Code, provided, however, that Lender shall give Borrower at least 10 days' notice before any sale of the Collateral.  Lender may, in its sole discretion, waive any Event of Default.  Lender shall be entitled to recover from Borrower the reasonable attorneys' fees that Lender incurs in enforcing this agreement.

K. **General.**  Colorado law governs this agreement.  This agreement is the entire agreement between the parties regarding the Loan.  The parties may amend this agreement only by a writing that both parties sign. Each notice under this agreement must be in writing and is effective when the recipient receives it.
