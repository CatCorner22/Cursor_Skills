# Marketing Services Agreement

This marketing services agreement is dated September 14, 2027, and is between Foxglove Creative Studio, LLC, a Georgia limited liability company ("**Agency**"), and Brambleton Coffee Roasters, LLC, a Georgia limited liability company ("**Client**").

The parties agree as follows:

A. **Definitions.**  In this agreement:

1. "**Approved Budget**" means the budget for the campaign in Schedule A, which Client shall approve in writing before Agency begins work.

2. "**Campaign Materials**" means the ads, social-media posts, videos, and landing pages that Agency creates for the campaign.

3. "**Influencer**" means an individual whom Agency engages to promote Client's products on social media.

B. **Services.**

1. Agency shall plan, create, and run the digital marketing campaign for Client's Winter Hollow Blend coffee that Schedule A describes.

2. Agency will submit each item of Campaign Materials to Client for approval at least 5 business days before its scheduled release.

3. Client should review each item of Campaign Materials for factual accuracy before approving it.

4. Agency shall not release any item of Campaign Materials that Client has not approved.

C. **Influencers.**

1. Agency may engage an Influencer for the campaign only with Client's approval of that Influencer.

2. Each Influencer must clearly disclose the Influencer's relationship with Client in each sponsored post, as 16 C.F.R. Part 255 requires.

3. Agency shall include in each agreement with an Influencer a provision requiring that disclosure.

D. **Budget.**

1. Agency shall not incur third-party media costs in excess of the approved budget without Client's written consent.

2. If Agency should exceed the Approved Budget, Agency shall bear the excess cost unless Client consented to the excess in writing.

3. Client may, in its sole discretion, increase the Approved Budget by notice to Agency.

E. **Fees and Payment.**

1. Agency is entitled to receive a monthly retainer of $8,500 for each month of the term.

2. Agency shall invoice Client for each month's retainer, and for the third-party media costs that Agency paid in the preceding month, by the 5th day of each month.

3. Client shall pay each invoice no later than 30 days after Client receives it.

F. **Ownership.**  When Client has paid all amounts due for an item of Campaign Materials, Client owns all rights in that item.  For the avoidance of doubt, Agency retains ownership of its pre-existing templates and software tools.

G. **Term and Termination.**

1. This agreement shall terminate on December 31, 2027, unless either party terminates it earlier under this section.

2. Either party may terminate this agreement by giving the other party at least 30 days' notice.

3. Client shall terminate the campaign if the U.S. Food and Drug Administration or the Georgia Department of Agriculture announces a recall of any product that the campaign promotes, and Client shall notify Agency of that termination no later than 1 business day after the announcement.

4. If Client terminates the campaign, Agency shall stop all paid placements no later than 24 hours after Agency receives Client's notice of termination.

H. **General.**

1. Georgia law governs this agreement.

2. Neither party may assign this agreement without the other party's written consent.

3. Each notice under this agreement must be in writing and is effective when the recipient receives it.

4. This agreement and Schedule A are the entire agreement between the parties regarding the campaign.
