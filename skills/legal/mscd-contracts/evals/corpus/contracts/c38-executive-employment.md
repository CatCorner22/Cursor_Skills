# EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This agreement is dated April 5, 2027, and is between Northgate Freight Analytics, Inc., an Ohio corporation (**"Company"**), and Danielle Okonkwo-Reyes (**"Executive"**).

A. **Position and Duties.**  Company shall employ Executive as Company's Chief Operating Officer, reporting to Company's Chief Executive Officer.  Executive shall devote Executive's full business time to Company's business, except that Executive may serve on the board of 1 nonprofit organization.

B. **Term.**  The term of Executive's employment under this agreement (the **"Term"**) starts on May 3, 2027, and ends on May 2, 2030, unless Company or Executive ends Executive's employment earlier under the section titled "Termination."

C. **Compensation.**

1. Company shall pay Executive a base salary of $385,000 per year (**"Base Salary"**) in accordance with Company's regular payroll practices.

2. Company shall pay Executive a one-time signing bonus of $60,000 no later than 30 days after the start of the Term.  For the avoidance of doubt, the signing bonus is not part of Base Salary for any purpose.

3. Executive is eligible to participate in Company's annual incentive plan, with a target bonus of 50% of Base Salary.

D. **Termination.**

1. Company shall have the right to terminate Executive's employment at any time, with or without Cause.  **"Cause"** means Executive's conviction of a felony, Executive's willful misconduct that harms Company, or Executive's breach of the section titled "Restrictive Covenants."

2. Executive must give Company at least 60 days' notice before resigning.

3. If Company terminates Executive's employment without Cause, Company shall pay Executive severance equal to 12 months of Base Salary in equal installments on Company's regular payroll dates during the 12 months after the termination date, on condition that Executive signs, and does not revoke, a release of claims in a form that Company provides.  Each installment is a separate payment for purposes of Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code.  Executive is not required to mitigate the amount of any severance payment by seeking other employment.

E. **Restrictive Covenants.**

1. **Noncompetition.**  During Executive's employment and for twelve (12) months after it ends, Executive shall not, anywhere in the United States, engage in any business that is substantially similar to Company's business of freight-rate analytics software; provided, however, that Executive may own up to 2% of the outstanding stock of any publicly traded company.

2. **Nonsolicitation.**  During Executive's employment and for 12 months after it ends, Executive shall not solicit (a) any customer that Executive knows or should have known is a customer of Company, for the purpose of selling freight-rate analytics software to that customer, or (b) any employee of Company to leave Company's employment, unless he or she is responding to a general job advertisement that does not target Company's employees.

3. **Confidential Information.**  **"Confidential Information"** means all nonpublic information about Company's business, customers, pricing, and technology that Executive receives during Executive's employment.  Executive shall not use or disclose any Confidential Information except as Executive's duties for Company require.  When Executive's employment ends, Executive shall return to Company all documents and devices that contain Proprietary Information.

F. **General.**

1. Ohio law governs this agreement.  Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the United States District Court for the Southern District of Ohio for any dispute under this agreement and, for any appeal, the United States Court of Appeals for the Sixth Circuit.

2. All provisions of this agreement that by their nature should survive termination shall survive.

3. This agreement is the parties' entire agreement on its subject matter, and the parties may amend it only in a writing that both of them sign.
