Re: Joint Venture for the Desert Basin Water Authority Meter Replacement Project

Dear Ms. Okafor:

This letter agreement states the terms of a joint venture between Saguaro Flow Systems, Inc. ("Saguaro") and Pennant Utility Services, LLC ("Pennant") to bid on and perform the advanced-metering replacement project that the Desert Basin Water Authority (the "Authority") described in its Request for Proposals No. 27-114 (the "Project").

1. Formation of the Venture.  Saguaro and Pennant form an unincorporated joint venture (the "Venture") for the sole purpose of submitting a joint proposal for the Project and, if the Authority awards the Project to the Venture, performing the Project.  Neither party may bind the Venture or the other party without the approval of the management committee.

2. Contributions.  Each party agrees to contribute 50% of the cost of preparing the proposal prior to the proposal due date of May 20, 2027.  If the Authority awards the Project to the Venture, Saguaro will supply the meters, radio modules, and data-collection software for the Project, and Pennant will provide the installation crews, vehicles, and field supervision.

3. Profits and Losses.  The parties shall share the profits and losses of the Venture fifty-five percent (55%) to Saguaro and 45% to Pennant.

4. Management.  The parties shall manage the Venture through a management committee.  Each party shall appoint 2 members of the committee.  Each decision of the committee requires the vote of all 4 members.

5. Indemnification.  Each party shall indemnify and hold harmless the other party against any claim arising from the indemnifying party's negligence in performing its obligations hereunder.

6. Withdrawal.  To withdraw from the Venture before the Authority awards the Project, a party must give the other party at least 15 days' notice.  After the Authority awards the Project, neither party may withdraw until the Venture has completed the Project.

7. Proposal Price.  The final proposal price requires the approval of the management committee under paragraph 4 above.

8. Term.  This letter agreement terminates on the earliest of (a) the date on which the Authority awards the Project to another bidder, (b) the date on which the Venture completes the Project, and (c) the date on which a party withdraws pursuant to Section 6.  Time is of the essence in the performance of each party's obligations under this letter agreement.

9. General.  Arizona law governs this letter agreement.  Each party shall bear its own costs of performing this letter agreement.  This letter agreement is binding on the parties and their respective successors and assigns.  Headings in this letter agreement are for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.  The parties may amend this letter agreement only by a writing that both parties sign.
