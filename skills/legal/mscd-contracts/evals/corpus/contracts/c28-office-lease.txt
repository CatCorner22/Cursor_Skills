LEASE

THIS LEASE (the "Lease") is dated this ___ day of __________, 2027, and is between Castellan Tower Associates LLC, a New York limited liability company ("Landlord"), and Pellworth Textile Design, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Tenant").

WHEREAS, Landlord owns One Liberty Plaza, an office building in New York, New York (the "Building"); and

WHEREAS, Tenant wishes to lease office space in the Building from Landlord.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, Landlord and Tenant agree as follows:

A. Premises.  Landlord leases to Tenant, and Tenant leases from Landlord, Suite 2210 on the 22nd floor of the Building, containing about 6,200 rentable square feet (the "Premises").  Tenant may use the lobbies, elevators, and restrooms of the Building in common with the other tenants of the Building.

B. Term.  The term of this Lease shall commence on March 1, 2027, and shall end on February 29, 2028 (the "Expiration Date"), unless Tenant extends the term under the section titled "Option to Extend."

C. Rent.  Tenant shall pay Landlord base rent of Twelve Thousand Four Hundred Dollars ($12,400) per month ("Base Rent").  Base Rent shall be paid in advance on the first day of each month, without demand, at Landlord's office or at any other place that Landlord designates by notice to Tenant.  If any installment of Base Rent remains unpaid 5 days after its due date, Tenant shall pay Landlord a late charge of five percent (5%) of the unpaid installment.

D. Security Deposit.  When Tenant signs this Lease, Tenant shall deposit $24,800 with Landlord as security for Tenant's performance of this Lease (the "Security Deposit").  Landlord shall return the Security Deposit to Tenant, less any amount that Landlord applies to cure a default by Tenant, within 90 days of the Expiration Date.

E. Use and Repair.  Tenant shall use the Premises only for general office purposes.  Tenant shall keep the Premises in good repair and, at the end of the term, shall surrender the same to Landlord broom-clean, except for ordinary wear and tear.  Tenant is not required to remove any alteration that Landlord approved in writing.

F. Services.  Landlord will furnish heat, ventilation, and air conditioning to the Premises on each weekday that is not a federal holiday.  Landlord shall keep the roof, the structure, and the mechanical systems of the Building in good repair.

G. Option to Extend.  Tenant may extend the term for 1 additional period of 2 years by giving Landlord notice no later than 180 days before the Expiration Date.  Base Rent for the extension period is 103% of the Base Rent in effect on the Expiration Date.

H. Default.  If Tenant shall fail to pay any installment of Base Rent no later than 10 days after Tenant receives notice of nonpayment from Landlord, Landlord may terminate this Lease by giving Tenant notice, and Tenant shall pay Landlord any and all amounts due hereunder through the termination date.  Landlord may also apply the Security Deposit as described in Section D.

I. General.  New York law governs this Lease.  Each notice under this Lease must be in writing and is effective when the recipient receives it.  Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Premises without Landlord's written consent.  This Lease is the entire agreement between Landlord and Tenant regarding the Premises.
