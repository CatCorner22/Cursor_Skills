# Operating Agreement of Saltmarsh Holdings, LLC (Excerpt)

*[Articles on management, allocations, distributions, and dissolution omitted.]*

This operating agreement is dated March 3, 2027, and is between Saltmarsh Holdings, LLC, a South Carolina limited liability company ("Company"), and Calvin Ashby, Renata Duvall-Marsh, and Lowcountry Timber Partners, LP, a South Carolina limited partnership (each, a "Member").

## I. DEFINITIONS

A. "Capital Contribution" means money that a Member contributes to Company.

B. "Percentage Interest" means, for each Member, the number of Units that the Member holds divided by the total number of outstanding Units.

C. "Units" means the units of membership interest in Company.

## II. TRANSFERS

A. **Restriction.**  A Member may transfer Units only in compliance with this article.

B. **Right of First Refusal.**

1. If a Member (the "Offering Member") wishes to sell Units to a third party, the Offering Member shall give Company and the other Members a notice (the "Offer Notice") that states the number of Units, the price, and the identity of the proposed buyer.

2. Company and the other Members have a right of first refusal to buy the offered Units at the price in the Offer Notice.  Company or any other Member may exercise that right by notice to the Offering Member no later than 30 days after receiving the Offer Notice.

3. If neither Company nor any other Member exercises the right of first refusal within 20 days after receiving the Offer Notice, the Offering Member may sell the offered Units to the proposed buyer at a price no lower than the price in the Offer Notice.

C. **Joinder.**  Each transferee shall sign a joinder to this agreement before the transfer takes effect.

## III. CAPITAL CALLS

A. **Calls.**  Company may require the Members to make Capital Contributions by giving them a written notice (each, a "Capital Call"), but only to fund operating shortfalls or substantial capital improvements, and Capital Calls may not exceed $250,000 in any fiscal year.

B. **Funding.**  No later than 15 days after Company gives a Capital Call, each Member shall contribute the amount of the Capital Call multiplied by that Member's Percentage Interest.

C. **Default.**  If a Member fails to contribute the amount that a Capital Call requires of that Member, any other Member may contribute the shortfall, and Company shall then cancel 1 of its Units for each $1,000 of the shortfall and issue 1 Unit to the contributing Member for each $1,000 that the contributing Member contributes.

D. **Capital Accounts.**  Company shall maintain a capital account for each Member under Section 1.704-1(b)(2)(iv) of the Treasury Regulations.

E. **Deposits.**  Company shall deposit each Capital Contribution in Company's operating account at First Citizens Bank.

F. **No Other Contributions.**  A Member is not required to make any Capital Contribution except under this article.
