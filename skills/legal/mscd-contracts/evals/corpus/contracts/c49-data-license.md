# Data License Agreement

This data license agreement is dated August 16, 2027, and is between Prairie Signal Analytics, Inc., a Kansas corporation ("**Licensor**"), and Sandhill Mutual Hail Underwriters, LLC, an Iowa limited liability company ("**Licensee**").

The parties agree as follows:

A. **Definitions.**  The following definitions apply in this agreement:

"**Authorized User**" means an employee or individual contractor of Licensee whom Licensee has authorized to access the Licensed Data.

"**Derived Data**" means any data, model output, or analysis that Licensee creates using the Licensed Data.

"**Licensed Data**" means Licensor's 2026 County Soil Moisture and Yield Dataset, including each Quarterly Update.

"**Licensee Data**" means all data that Licensee creates or collects in operating its business, including its policyholder records and loss models.

"**Quarterly Update**" means a revised version of Licensor's 2026 County Soil Moisture and Yield Dataset that adds the soil-moisture readings for the most recent calendar quarter.

B. **License.**

1. Licensor grants Licensee a nonexclusive, nontransferable license, during the Term, to use the Licensed Data to underwrite and price crop-hail insurance policies in Kansas, Nebraska, and Iowa.

2. Licensee shall not sell, sublicense, or disclose the Licensed Data to any person other than its Authorized Users.

3. Licensee shall not use the Licensed Data to create a dataset that is substantially similar to the Licensed Data.

4. Licensee is liable for any act or omission of an Authorized User that would breach this agreement if Licensee had committed it.

C. **Delivery.**

1. Licensor shall deliver the files in Schedule A to Licensee by secure file transfer no later than 5 days after the date of this agreement.

2. Licensor shall deliver each Quarterly Update within 15 days of the end of the calendar quarter that it covers.

3. Licensor is not required to customize the Licensed Data for Licensee.

D. **Fees.**  Licensee shall pay Licensor an annual license fee of $84,000 in 4 equal quarterly installments of $21,000, each no later than 30 days after Licensee receives Licensor's invoice for that installment.

E. **Ownership.**  Licensor owns the Licensed Data and all Derived Data.  Licensee owns all Licensee Data.

F. **Confidentiality.**  Each party (the "**Receiving Party**") shall keep confidential all nonpublic information that the other party (the "**Disclosing Party**") discloses to it under this agreement, and the Receiving Party shall use that information only to perform its obligations and exercise its rights under this agreement.

G. **Term and Termination.**

1. The term of this agreement (the "**Term**") begins on the date of this agreement and ends on December 31, 2028.

2. Either party may terminate this agreement by notice to the other party if the other party breaches this agreement and does not cure the breach no later than 30 days after receiving notice of the breach.

3. No later than 30 days after this agreement terminates or expires, Licensee shall destroy all copies of the Licensed Data in its possession and deliver to Licensor a certificate of destruction in the form of Exhibit 1.

H. **Governing Law.**  Kansas law governs this agreement.
