EVENT SPONSORSHIP AGREEMENT

This agreement is dated May 10, 2027, and is between Riverbend Arts Collective, Inc., a Missouri nonprofit corporation ("Organizer"), and Thistledown Craft Soda Co., a Missouri corporation ("Sponsor").

A. THE FESTIVAL

Organizer shall produce the Riverbend Lantern Festival at the Third Street Pavilion in Kansas City, Missouri, on September 18, 2027, and September 19, 2027 (the "Festival").

B. SPONSORSHIP FEE

1. Sponsor shall pay Organizer a sponsorship fee of $30,000 (the "Sponsorship Fee") in 2 installments: $15,000 no later than June 15, 2027, and $15,000 no later than September 1, 2027.

2. If Sponsor shall fail to pay either installment when due, Organizer may remove Sponsor's name from Festival advertising.

C. SPONSOR BENEFITS

1. Organizer shall display Sponsor's name and logo in all Festival advertising.

2. Sponsor is entitled to 20 complimentary tickets for each Festival day.

3. Sponsor may only sell its beverages at the 2 booths that Organizer designates for Sponsor.

4. Sponsor shall deliver its banners to Organizer no later than 5:00 p.m. Central time on September 10, 2027, and Organizer shall display those banners at the main stage and at each Festival entrance.

D. EXCLUSIVITY

1. Organizer shall not permit any beverage company other than Sponsor to provide funding for, advertise at, or sell products at the Festival.

2. Organizer may accept funding from companies in other industries, and Organizer may display the logo of Big Muddy Brewing Company, the Festival's beer vendor, on the beer tent.

E. CANCELLATION

If the City of Kansas City revokes Organizer's event permit or Organizer cancels the Festival for any other reason, it shall refund the Sponsorship Fee to Sponsor no later than 30 days after the cancellation.

F. CONFIDENTIALITY

1. "Confidential Information" means non-public information that a party (the "Disclosing Party") gives the other party (the "Receiving Party") in connection with the Festival.

2. The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information only to perform this agreement.

G. GENERAL

1. The maximum amount of each party's liability to the other party for claims arising out of this agreement is $25,000.

2. Missouri law governs this agreement.
