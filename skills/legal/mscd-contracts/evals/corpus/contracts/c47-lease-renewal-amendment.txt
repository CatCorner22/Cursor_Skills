AMENDMENT TO LEASE

This amendment to lease is dated January 8, 2027, and is between Chattahoochee Commons Owner, LLC, a Georgia limited liability company ("Landlord"), and Brookhaven Pediatric Therapy Group, P.C., a Georgia professional corporation ("Tenant").

Landlord and Tenant signed a lease on March 15, 2018 (the "Lease"), under which Tenant leases Suite 240 of the office building at 3350 Riverwood Parkway, Atlanta, Georgia (the "Premises").  The term of the Lease began on March 1, 2018, and ends on February 29, 2028 (the "Expiration Date").  Landlord and Tenant wish to give Tenant an option to renew the term of the Lease.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

A. Defined Terms.  Each capitalized term that this amendment uses but does not define has the meaning that the Lease gives it.

B. Renewal Option.

   1. Landlord grants Tenant an option (the "Renewal Option") to extend the term of the Lease for 1 period of 5 years beginning on March 1, 2028, and ending on February 28, 2033 (the "Renewal Term").

   2. To exercise the Renewal Option, Tenant must give Landlord notice of exercise (the "Renewal Notice") no later than 180 days before the Expiration Date (that is, no later than Friday, September 2, 2027).

   3. Tenant may exercise the Renewal Option only if no Event of Default exists on the date on which Tenant gives the Renewal Notice.

   4. If Tenant should exercise the Renewal Option, all provisions of the Lease apply during the Renewal Term, except that the section of this amendment titled "Renewal Rent" governs Base Rent for the Renewal Term and Tenant has no further option to renew.

   5. The Renewal Option is personal to Tenant, and no assignee or subtenant of Tenant may exercise it.

C. Renewal Rent.

   1. The annual Base Rent for the Renewal Term is the greater of 103% of the prior rent or fair market rent determined by an MAI appraiser that Landlord and Tenant jointly select.

   2. If Landlord and Tenant do not jointly select an appraiser within 90 days of the Expiration Date, Landlord and Tenant shall each select an appraiser no later than 10 days after that period ends, and Landlord and Tenant shall cause those 2 appraisers to select a 3rd appraiser, whose determination of fair market rent binds both parties.

   3. Each party shall pay the fees of the appraiser that it selects and 50% of the fees of any jointly selected appraiser or 3rd appraiser.

   4. Tenant shall pay Base Rent for the Renewal Term in equal monthly installments, in advance, on the 1st day of each month.

D. Condition of Premises.  Tenant shall accept the Premises for the Renewal Term in their condition on the Expiration Date, and Landlord is not required to make any improvements to the Premises or give Tenant any improvement allowance for the Renewal Term.

E. Tenant's Statements.  Tenant states that the Lease is in effect, that Tenant has not assigned the Lease or sublet any part of the Premises, and that Tenant has not given Landlord any notice of default under the Lease that remains uncured.

F. Effect of Amendment.  Except as this amendment provides, the Lease remains in effect without change.  If this amendment conflicts with the Lease, this amendment governs.

G. Counterparts.  The parties may sign this amendment in counterparts, including by electronic signature, and all counterparts together constitute a single instrument.

The parties are signing this amendment on the date in the introductory clause.
