# SALES REPRESENTATIVE AGREEMENT

This sales representative agreement is made this ___ day of ________, 2027, between Corvane Marine Supply, Inc., a Florida corporation (the "Principal"), and Pell Harbor Marketing LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("Representative").

WHEREAS, the Principal manufactures and sells marine hardware; and

WHEREAS, Representative has experience selling marine hardware to retail chandleries in Florida;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

## A. Appointment

1. The Principal hereby appoints Representative as its strategic partner and exclusive sales representative for the Principal's marine hardware products (the "Products") in the Florida counties in Schedule A (the "Territory").

2. Representative will solicit orders for the Products only within said Territory and only from retail chandleries.

3. No one other than Representative may solicit orders for the Products in the Territory on the Principal's behalf.

## B. Duties of Representative

1. Representative shall use commercially reasonable best efforts to promote the Products in the Territory.

2. Representative shall submit each order to the Principal for the Principal's consideration.  The Principal may, in its sole discretion, accept or reject any order.

3. Representative should notify the Principal of any customer complaint about the Products.

4. Representative shall keep a substantial inventory of demonstration Products at its office.

## C. Commissions

1. The Principal shall pay Representative a commission of five percent (5%) of the amount that each customer in the Territory pays the Principal for Products that Representative sold to that customer.

2. The Principal shall pay Representative each commission no later than 15 days after the end of the month in which the Principal receives the customer's payment, provided that the customer has paid for the order in full.

3. Representative is responsible for all of its own expenses, including travel and samples, and the Principal is not required to reimburse them.

## D. Term and Termination

1. This agreement continues until either party terminates it by giving the other party notice at least 60 days before the termination date that the notice specifies.

2. On termination, the Principal shall pay Representative, as provided in Section C above, the commission on each order that the Principal accepted before the termination date.

3. Representative shall return all samples and/or sales literature to the Principal no later than 10 days after termination.

## E. Assignment

1. Any assignment of this agreement by Representative without the Principal's prior written consent is null and void.

## F. General

1. Florida law governs this agreement.

2. Each party shall give notices pursuant to this agreement in writing, by hand delivery or by certified mail, to the other party's address below its signature.

3. This agreement is the parties' entire agreement on its subject.
