# SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT AND RELEASE

This Settlement Agreement and Release (this "Agreement") is dated February 2, 2027, and is between Gulfstream Hospitality Group, LLC, a Florida limited liability company (the "Owner"), and Coastal Kitchen Installations, Inc., a Georgia corporation (the "Installer").

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, on 14 September 2026, the Owner sued the Installer in the Circuit Court for Duval County, Florida, Case No. 2026-CA-004417 (the "Lawsuit"), alleging defects in commercial kitchen equipment that the Installer installed at the Owner's restaurant at Two Riverside Place, Jacksonville, Florida; and

WHEREAS, the parties wish to settle the Lawsuit without any admission of liability;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises herein, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. SETTLEMENT PAYMENT

1.1 The Installer will pay the Owner Forty-Two Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($42,500.00) (the "Settlement Amount") within ten (10) days of the date of this Agreement.

1.2 The Installer shall pay the Settlement Amount by wire transfer to the trust account of the Owner's counsel, Marlowe & Pike, P.A.  The Settlement Amount is a one-time payment, and the Installer has no other payment obligation under this Agreement.

## 2. RELEASES

2.1 The Owner releases the Installer from any and all claims arising out of or relating to the Lawsuit or the kitchen equipment, including but not limited to claims for breach of warranty, negligence, and delay damages.

2.2 The Owner's release does not extend to any latent defect that the Installer knows or should have known about and did not disclose to the Owner before the date of this Agreement.

2.3 The Installer releases the Owner from all claims arising out of the Lawsuit, and the Installer shall not assert the same in any later proceeding.

2.4 The parties acknowledge and agree that the Owner shall file a stipulation of dismissal with prejudice in the form of Exhibit A no later than 5 days after the Owner receives the Settlement Amount.

## 3. CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-DISPARAGEMENT

3.1 The terms of this Agreement shall be kept confidential, except for disclosures to a party's attorneys, accountants, and insurers.

3.2 No party shall make any statement that disparages another party regarding the Lawsuit.

## 4. GENERAL

4.1 Each party shall bear its own attorneys' fees and costs, and each party waives any right to recover fees under Section 768.79 of the Florida Statutes.

4.2 This Agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and assigns.

4.3 Entire agreement:  This Agreement is the parties' entire agreement regarding the Lawsuit.

4.4 Florida law governs this Agreement.
