# CREDIT AGREEMENT (EXCERPT)

This agreement is dated March 15, 2027, and is between Cumberland Gap Equipment Rentals, LLC, a Kentucky limited liability company (**"Borrower"**), and Larchmont Commercial Finance, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (**"Lender"**).

## I. DEFINITIONS AND ACCOUNTING TERMS

A. **Defined Terms.**  In this agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

**"Capital Expenditures"** means, for any period, Borrower's expenditures during that period for property, plant, or equipment that GAAP requires Borrower to capitalize.

**"Collection Account"** means Borrower's deposit account at Fifth Third Bank with an account number ending in 4417.

**"Compliance Certificate"** means a certificate that Borrower's chief financial officer signs and that shows Borrower's calculation of the Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio and the Leverage Ratio as of the last day of the most recent Fiscal Quarter, and Borrower shall deliver a Compliance Certificate to Lender with each set of financial statements that Borrower delivers under the section titled "Financial Statements."

**"EBITDA"** means, for any period, Net Income for that period plus Borrower's interest expense, income tax expense, depreciation expense, and amortization expense for that period, in each case to the extent that Borrower deducted that expense in calculating Net Income for that period.

**"Fiscal Quarter"** means each 3-month period that ends on March 31, June 30, September 30, or December 31.

**"Fiscal Year"** means each 52- or 53-week period that ends on the last Saturday of September.

**"Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio"** means, for any period of 4 consecutive Fiscal Quarters, the ratio of (a) EBITDA for that period minus Capital Expenditures for that period to (b) Fixed Charges for that period.

**"Fixed Charges"** means, for any period, the sum of Borrower's cash interest expense for that period and the scheduled principal payments on Borrower's Indebtedness that come due during that period.

**"GAAP"** means United States generally accepted accounting principles as in effect from time to time.

**"Indebtedness"** means all of Borrower's obligations for borrowed money, all of Borrower's obligations under capital leases, and all of Borrower's guaranties of any other Person's obligations for borrowed money.

**"Leverage Ratio"** means, on any date, the ratio of Borrower's Indebtedness on that date to EBITDA for the 4 consecutive Fiscal Quarters that most recently ended on or before that date.

**"Lien"** means any mortgage, pledge, security interest, encumbrance, or other charge on property that secures an obligation.

**"Net Income"** means, for any period, Borrower's net income for that period under GAAP.

**"Permitted Liens"** means Liens that the section titled "Liens" permits.

**"Person"** means an individual, an entity, or a governmental authority.

**"Restricted Payment"** means any dividend or other distribution on Borrower's equity interests, and any payment to redeem or buy back any of Borrower's equity interests.

B. **Accounting Terms.**  Each accounting term that this agreement uses but does not define has the meaning that GAAP gives it, and Borrower shall prepare all financial statements and calculations under this agreement in accordance with GAAP on a consistent basis.

## II. COVENANTS

A. **Financial Statements.**  Borrower shall deliver to Lender:

1. no later than 120 days after the end of each Fiscal Year, Borrower's annual financial statements for that Fiscal Year, together with an audit report on those statements from an independent certified public accounting firm that Lender approves; and

2. no later than 45 days after the end of each Fiscal Quarter, Borrower's unaudited financial statements for that Fiscal Quarter.

B. **Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio.**  Borrower shall not permit the Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio for any period of 4 consecutive Fiscal Quarters to be less than 1.25 to 1.00.

C. **Leverage Ratio.**  Borrower shall not permit the Leverage Ratio on the last day of any Fiscal Quarter to exceed 3.50 to 1.00.

D. **Indebtedness.**  Borrower shall not incur or allow to exist any Indebtedness other than (a) Borrower's obligations to Lender under this agreement and (b) obligations under capital leases that do not exceed $4,000,000 in total outstanding at any time.

E. **Liens.**  Borrower shall not create or allow to exist any Lien on any of Borrower's property other than Permitted Liens.

F. **Restricted Payments.**  Borrower shall not make any Restricted Payment, except that Borrower may make Restricted Payments of not more than $2,500,000 if Borrower is in compliance with the sections titled "Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio" and "Leverage Ratio" on the date of each Restricted Payment.

G. **Collection Account.**  Borrower shall instruct each of Borrower's rental customers to pay all rental fees into the Collection Account, and Borrower shall not change the account into which any customer pays rental fees without Lender's written consent.

H. **Fiscal Year.**  Borrower shall not change Borrower's Fiscal Year.
