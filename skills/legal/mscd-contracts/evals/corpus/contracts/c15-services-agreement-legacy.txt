PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Professional Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is effective as of 3/1/2027 (the "Effective Date") and is between Tallgrass Regional Medical Center, Inc., a Kansas nonprofit corporation (the "Client"), and Meridian Point Consulting, LLC, a Georgia limited liability company (the "Consultant").

The parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS

(a) "Services" shall mean the revenue-cycle consulting services in Appendix A attached hereto.

(b) "Deliverables" means the reports, plans, and training materials that the Consultant prepares for the Client under this Agreement.

2. SERVICES

(a) The Consultant shall execute the Services at the Client's hospital in Wichita, Kansas, and remotely.

(b) The Consultant shall have the right to subcontract any part of the Services with the Client's written consent.

(c) The Client is not required to provide office space or equipment to the Consultant's personnel.

3. FEES AND EXPENSES

(a) The Client must pay the Consultant $185 per hour for the Services, plus a one-time onboarding fee of $2,500.

(b) The Client shall reimburse travel expenses incurred by the Consultant, provided, however, that the Consultant obtains the Client's written approval before incurring any single expense over $500.

(c) The Consultant shall invoice the Client monthly, and the Client shall pay each invoice no later than fifteen (15) days after the day on which the Client receives it.

4. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR

It is understood and agreed that the Consultant is an independent contractor and not an employee of the Client. The Consultant's personnel shall comply with the Client's safety and patient-privacy policies while on the Client's premises.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY AND OWNERSHIP

(a) The Consultant shall keep confidential all patient information and other non-public information of the Client and/or its affiliates that the Consultant obtains in performing the Services.

(b) The Deliverables are works made for hire under the Copyright Act, and the Client owns all rights in the Deliverables.

6. TERMINATION

(a) Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time by notice to the other party.

(b) On termination, the Client shall pay the Consultant all fees that accrued through the termination date under Section 3(a) above.

(c) All provisions of this Agreement that by their nature should survive termination shall survive termination.

7. GENERAL

(a) The parties may amend the terms and conditions of this Agreement only by a writing that both parties sign.

(b) Kansas law governs this Agreement.

SERVICES
