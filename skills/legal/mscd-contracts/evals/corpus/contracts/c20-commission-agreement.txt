SALES COMMISSION AGREEMENT

This Sales Commission Agreement (this "Agreement") is dated April 1, 2027, and is between Larkspur Solar Systems, Inc., a Nevada corporation ("Company"), and Devon Achterberg ("Representative").

A. DEFINITIONS

1. "Net Revenue" means the amount that a customer pays Company for a solar installation, minus sales tax, permit fees, and any refund.

2. "Service Contract" means a multi-year maintenance contract for a solar installation.

B. COMMISSIONS

1. Representative is entitled to a commission on each Qualified Sale that Representative closes while this Agreement is in effect.

2. The commission rate shall be 6% of the Net Revenue from each Qualified Sale.

3. Company shall pay Representative a bonus of $2,000 for each sale of a battery-storage system or a Service Contract exceeding $50,000.

4. Company shall pay each commission on the fifteenth day of the month after the month in which the customer pays Company in full.

5. Company intends each commission payment to qualify as a short-term deferral under Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code.

C. ACCELERATOR

1. For Qualified Sales that Representative closes from January 1 to March 31 of each year, the commission rate is 8% instead of 6%.

2. Representative should enter each Qualified Sale in Company's customer-relationship-management system no later than 2 days after closing that sale.

D. TERRITORY

1. Representative's territory is Clark County, Nevada.

2. Company may, in its sole discretion, change Representative's territory by notice to Representative.

3. Representative shall offer the products of Company and/or its affiliates only to customers in Representative's territory.

E. CHARGEBACKS

1. If a customer cancels a Qualified Sale and receives a refund no later than 90 days after installation, Representative agrees to repay the related commission to Company.

2. Company may deduct any repayment from one or more later commission payments to Representative.

F. TERM AND TERMINATION

1. Either party may terminate this Agreement by notice to the other party, effective 30 days after the other party receives the notice.

2. After termination, Company shall pay Representative commissions on Qualified Sales that Representative closed before the termination date, on the schedule in the section titled "Commissions."

3. Company is not required to pay any commission on a sale that Representative closes after the termination date.

G. GENERAL

1. Nevada law governs this Agreement.

2. This Agreement is the parties' entire agreement regarding commissions. The parties may amend this Agreement only in a writing that both parties sign.
