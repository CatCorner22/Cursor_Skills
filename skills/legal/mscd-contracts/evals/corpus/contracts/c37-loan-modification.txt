COMMERCIAL LOAN MODIFICATION AGREEMENT

This agreement is dated January 22, 2027, and is between Bluestem Capital Partners, LLC, a Missouri limited liability company ("Lender"); Ozark Ridge Hospitality, LLC, an Arkansas limited liability company ("Borrower"); and Wendell P. Garrity, an individual who resides in Benton County, Arkansas ("Guarantor").

BACKGROUND

Lender made a loan to Borrower in the original principal amount of $4,850,000 (the "Loan") under a loan agreement between Lender and Borrower dated June 14, 2023 (the "Loan Agreement").  To evidence the Loan, Borrower signed a promissory note dated June 14, 2023, payable to Lender (the "Note").  Guarantor guaranteed payment of the Loan under a guaranty dated June 14, 2023 (the "Guaranty").

Borrower is renovating the Quillfeather Inn in Eureka Springs, Arkansas (the "Hotel"), and has asked Lender to reduce Borrower's monthly payments during the renovation.  Lender is willing to do so on the terms of this agreement.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

A. Definitions.  A capitalized term that this agreement uses but does not define has the meaning that the Loan Agreement gives it.  "Modification Period" means the period from March 1, 2027 to June 30, 2027.  "Payment Date" means the 1st day of each calendar month.

B. Interest and Payments.

1. Starting on the date of this agreement, the interest rate on the Loan shall be 6.25% per year.

2. On each Payment Date during the Modification Period, Borrower shall pay Lender all accrued and unpaid interest on the Loan.  Borrower is not required to pay principal on those Payment Dates.

3. Starting on the first Payment Date after the Modification Period, Borrower shall pay Lender monthly installments of principal and interest of $31,420 each.

C. Fees.  Borrower agrees to pay Lender a modification fee of $7,500 on the date of this agreement.  Borrower shall also reimburse Lender for the reasonable attorneys' fees that Lender incurs in preparing this agreement, no later than 10 days after Borrower receives Lender's invoice for those fees.

D. Renovation, Reporting, and Inspection.

1. Borrower shall deliver to Lender a renovation budget and construction schedule for the Hotel no later than February 29, 2027.  Borrower shall give Lender a copy of each renovation contract that exceeds $50,000 prior to signing it.

2. Borrower shall deliver to Lender Borrower's unaudited financial statements for the quarter ending March 31, 2027, by April 15, 2027.

3. Borrower shall make the Hotel available for Lender's inspection at 10:00 a.m. Central time on Monday, June 1, 2027.

E. Maturity Date.  The Maturity Date is Tuesday, February 29, 2028.  Borrower shall pay Lender all unpaid principal and accrued interest on the Loan on the Maturity Date.

F. Borrower's Statements.  Borrower represents and warrants to Lender that on the date of this agreement no Event of Default exists and Borrower has no defense, offset, or counterclaim to Borrower's obligations under the Loan Agreement or the Note.

G. Guarantor's Consent.  Guarantor consents to this agreement. Guarantor confirms that the Guaranty remains in effect and covers all of Borrower's obligations under the Loan Agreement, the Note, and this agreement.

H. Miscellaneous.  Except as this agreement states otherwise, the Loan Agreement, the Note, and the Guaranty remain in effect without change.  Arkansas law governs this agreement.  Time is of the essence of this agreement.  Each party may sign this agreement in counterparts and deliver its signature page by PDF, and each signature page that a party delivers by PDF has the effect of an original.
