# Source Code Escrow Agreement

This source code escrow agreement is dated April 12, 2027, and is between Tidewater Logic, Inc., a Virginia corporation ("**Depositor**"); Blue Ridge Freightworks, LLC, a Virginia limited liability company ("**Licensee**"); and Keystone Vault Escrow Services, LLC, a Pennsylvania limited liability company ("**Escrow Agent**").

Depositor licenses its HarborTrack warehouse-management software (the "**Software**") to Licensee under a software license agreement that Depositor and Licensee signed on January 4, 2027 (the "**License Agreement**").  The parties are entering into this agreement so that Licensee can obtain the source code for the Software if a Release Event occurs.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

A. **Definitions.**  The following definitions apply in this agreement:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday, or a federal holiday in the United States.

"**Critical Error**" has the meaning that the License Agreement gives it.

"**Deposit Materials**" means the Source Code and all other materials that Depositor deposits with Escrow Agent as Deposit Materials.

"**Documentation**" means the user manuals, administrator guides, and technical specifications for the Software.

"**Source Code**" means the human-readable form of the Software's computer code, including programmer comments and build scripts.

B. **Deposits.**

1. Depositor shall deposit the Deposit Materials with Escrow Agent no later than 10 Business Days after the date of this agreement.

2. Depositor shall deposit the Source Code for each new version of the Software no later than 30 days after Depositor makes that version available to Licensee.

3. Depositor states that the Deposit Materials, as of each deposit, include all Source Code that a programmer experienced in the Software's programming languages would need to compile the current version of the Software.

C. **Release Events.**

1. Each of the following is a "**Release Event**":

(a) Depositor files a voluntary petition under Chapter 7 of title 11 of the United States Code (the "**Bankruptcy Code**"), or a creditor files an involuntary petition against Depositor under that chapter and that petition remains pending 60 days after its filing.

(b) Depositor fails to correct a Critical Error or discontinues maintenance of the Software for more than 30 days.

(c) Depositor rejects the License Agreement in a case under the Bankruptcy Code.

2. To request a release, Licensee must deliver to Escrow Agent and Depositor a notice that identifies the Release Event that Licensee claims has occurred (a "**Release Request**").

D. **Release Procedure.**

1. Depositor may object to a Release Request by delivering to Escrow Agent and Licensee, no later than 15 Business Days after Depositor receives the Release Request, a notice that disputes the Release Event (a "**Contrary Notice**").

2. If Escrow Agent does not receive a Contrary Notice no later than 10 Business Days after Escrow Agent receives the Release Request, Escrow Agent shall deliver the Deposit Materials to Licensee.

3. If Escrow Agent receives a Contrary Notice before it delivers the Deposit Materials to Licensee, Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the Deposit Materials until Escrow Agent receives either joint written instructions from Depositor and Licensee or a copy of a final court order that directs the disposition of the Deposit Materials.  Escrow Agent shall then act on those instructions or that order.

E. **Use of Deposit Materials.**

1. Licensee may use the Deposit Materials that it receives under this agreement only to support and maintain the Software for Licensee's internal operations.

2. Except to support and maintain the Software, Licensee shall not copy, modify or distribute the Deposit Materials and the Documentation.

3. Licensee shall keep the Deposit Materials confidential and shall limit access to them to its employees and contractors who need that access to support and maintain the Software.

F. **Escrow Agent.**

1. Escrow Agent shall hold the Deposit Materials in a secure, climate-controlled vault and shall not release them to any person except as this agreement provides.

2. Escrow Agent is not required to verify the completeness, accuracy, or functionality of the Deposit Materials.

3. Escrow Agent represents and warrants that it maintains fidelity bond coverage of at least $1,000,000.

4. Escrow Agent is liable only for losses that its gross negligence or willful misconduct causes.

5. Licensee shall pay Escrow Agent a setup fee of $1,200 and an annual fee of $2,400, each no later than 30 days after Licensee receives Escrow Agent's invoice for that fee.

G. **Term.**  This agreement terminates when the License Agreement terminates.  No later than 30 days after this agreement terminates, Escrow Agent shall return the Deposit Materials to Depositor, unless Escrow Agent has already delivered them to Licensee under this agreement.

H. **General.**

1. The parties intend this agreement to be an "agreement supplementary to" the License Agreement for purposes of Section 365(n) of the Bankruptcy Code.

2. Virginia law governs this agreement.

The parties are signing this agreement on the date in the introductory clause.
