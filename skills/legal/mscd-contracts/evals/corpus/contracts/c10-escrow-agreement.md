# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This escrow agreement is dated March 1, 2027, and is between Hollis Creek Veterinary Partners, LLC, a Tennessee limited liability company ("Buyer"), and Wrenfield Animal Hospital, Inc., a Tennessee corporation ("Seller").

## Background

Buyer and Seller are parties to an asset purchase agreement dated February 15, 2027 (the "Purchase Agreement"), under which Buyer is buying the assets of Seller's veterinary clinic in Franklin, Tennessee.  The Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit $150,000 of the purchase price (the "Escrow Funds") with Cardinal Ridge Trust Company (the "Escrow Agent") as security for Seller's indemnification obligations under the Purchase Agreement.  The Escrow Agent is not a party to this agreement.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## A. Deposit and Investment

1. On the closing date under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Funds with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer.

2. The Escrow Agent must hold the Escrow Funds in a segregated account at First Horizon Bank and must not commingle the Escrow Funds with any other funds.

3. The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds only in United States Treasury bills that mature no more than 90 days after purchase.

## B. Claims

1. "Losses" has the meaning that the Purchase Agreement gives that term.

2. If Buyer claims indemnification for Losses under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a notice that states the amount of the claim and the facts on which Buyer bases the claim (a "Claim Notice").

3. Buyer may not recover more than $40,000 from the Escrow Funds for Losses that result from environmental conditions at the clinic.

4. "Objection Period" means the 15 days after Seller receives a Claim Notice.  Seller may dispute a Claim Notice by delivering to Buyer and the Escrow Agent, no later than 20 days after Seller receives the Claim Notice, a notice that disputes it (an "Objection Notice").

5. The Escrow Agent must pay Buyer the amount of each Claim Notice no later than 5 days after the day on which the Objection Period ends, unless Seller shall have delivered an Objection Notice before that day.

6. If Seller delivers an Objection Notice, the Escrow Agent must hold the disputed amount until the Escrow Agent receives either joint written instructions from Buyer and Seller or a copy of a final, nonappealable court order.

7. The Escrow Agent is not required to determine whether any Claim Notice or Objection Notice is valid.

## C. Release

1. On September 1, 2028, the Escrow Agent must release to Seller the Escrow Funds then remaining, minus the total amount of all Claim Notices that are then unresolved.

2. Neither Buyer nor Seller may instruct the Escrow Agent to release any Escrow Funds except by a joint written instruction or as this agreement provides.

## D. Fees

1. Buyer and Seller shall each pay 50% of the Escrow Agent's fees.

2. Seller shall reimburse Buyer for any additional fee that the Escrow Agent charges because of its failure to give a notice that this agreement requires.

## E. General

1. Tennessee law governs this agreement.

2. Buyer and Seller may amend this agreement only by a writing that both of them sign, and Buyer shall give the Escrow Agent a copy of each amendment.

3. This agreement terminates when the Escrow Agent has released all of the Escrow Funds.
