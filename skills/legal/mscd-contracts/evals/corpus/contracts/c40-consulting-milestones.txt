CONSULTING AGREEMENT

This agreement is dated May 10, 2027, and is between Palmetto Shoals Oyster Company, Inc., a South Carolina corporation ("Client"), and Ashby Hydrology Group, LLC, a Virginia limited liability company ("Consultant").

A. Services.  Consultant shall execute a hydrology and water quality study (the "Services") for Client's proposed oyster nursery at Client's lease site in Bulls Bay, South Carolina (the "Site").  The Services consist of preparing the 4 deliverables in the milestone table in the section titled "Fees and Milestones" (the "Deliverables").  Consultant shall perform the Services with the care and skill that hydrology consultants who practice in South Carolina customarily exercise.

B. Client Responsibilities.  Client will provide Consultant with access to the Site and to Client's boats and docks during normal working hours.  Client shall give Consultant copies of all water quality data for the Site that Client possesses, including but not limited to data from Client's 2025 and 2026 sampling programs.

C. Consultant Personnel.  Consultant shall designate Dr. Imogen Pryce as project lead for the Services and shall not replace her without Client's consent.  Consultant's employees shall comply with Client's site safety rules while they are at the Site.

D. Fees and Milestones.  Client shall pay Consultant the fee for each milestone in the following table after Client accepts the Deliverable for that milestone:

    Milestone   Deliverable                            Fee
    ---------   ------------------------------------   ---------------------------------
    1           Site survey and sampling plan          $9,500
    2           Baseline water quality data report     $14,000
    3           Draft hydrodynamic model               Twelve Thousand Dollars ($12,000)
    4           Final report and permit memorandum     $11,000
    ---------   ------------------------------------   ---------------------------------
    Total of all milestone fees: $48,000

Consultant shall deliver each Deliverable to Client by email.  Client shall review each Deliverable and either accept it or give Consultant notice of the Deliverable's deficiencies promptly after receiving it.  Client is not required to pay for any Deliverable that Client has not accepted.  Consultant shall not begin work on the second milestone until Client accepts the Deliverable for milestone 1.  Consultant shall invoice Client for each milestone after Client accepts the related Deliverable, and Client shall pay each invoice no later than 30 days after Client receives it.

E. Expenses.  Client shall reimburse Consultant for the travel expenses incurred by Consultant in performing the Services, up to $3,000 in total, if Consultant submits receipts for those expenses with Consultant's invoices.

F. Ownership of Deliverables.  Each Deliverable is a work made for hire for Client.  To the extent that any Deliverable does not qualify as a work made for hire, Consultant assigns to Client all of Consultant's rights in that Deliverable.  Client may use the Deliverables for permit applications, investor presentations, grant proposals, and/or regulatory filings.

G. Term and Termination.  This agreement ends when Client has paid Consultant for all 4 milestones.  Either party may terminate this agreement by giving the other party at least 15 days' notice.  If Client terminates this agreement, Client shall pay Consultant for the Services that Consultant performed before the termination took effect.

H. General.  The parties shall cooperate with one another in responding to any regulatory agency's questions about the Deliverables.  South Carolina law governs this agreement.  This agreement is binding on the parties and their respective successors and assigns.  This agreement is the parties' entire agreement on its subject matter.
