Re: Employment Offer

Dear Avery:

This letter agreement is between Brightfield Learning Co., a North Carolina corporation ("Brightfield"), and you.  It states the terms of your employment with Brightfield.

A. Position and Start Date

1. Brightfield shall employ you as Director of Curriculum, reporting to Brightfield's chief academic officer.  Your employment begins on February 1, 2027 (the "Start Date").

2. You will devote your full working time to Brightfield's business.

3. Your employment is at will.  Either you or Brightfield may end your employment at any time, with or without Cause.

B. Compensation

1. Your annual base salary (the "Base Salary") shall be $96,000, which Brightfield shall pay in accordance with its regular payroll practices.

2. The Base Salary will be reviewed each year.

3. You are eligible for an annual bonus of up to 15% of the Base Salary if you meet one or more of the bonus targets that Brightfield sets for that year. Brightfield is not required to pay any bonus for a year in which your employment ends before December 31 of that year.

4. Brightfield shall pay you a signing bonus of $10,000 (the "Signing Bonus") by March 15, 2027.  Neither you nor Brightfield shall disclose the amount of the Signing Bonus to any other Brightfield employee.

5. Brightfield shall grant you an option to buy 4,000 shares of Brightfield's common stock under the Brightfield 2026 Equity Incentive Plan.  The option vests as to 50% of the shares on the 1st anniversary of the Start Date and as to the remaining 50% on the second anniversary of the Start Date.

C. Benefits and Expenses

1. You may participate in the benefit plans that Brightfield offers to its employees generally, subject to the terms of those plans.

2. Brightfield shall reimburse you for business expenses that you incur in performing your duties, in accordance with Brightfield's expense policy.  Brightfield shall pay each reimbursement no later than December 31 of the year after the year in which you incur the expense, as Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code requires.

D. Termination

1. "Cause" means (a) your conviction of a felony; (b) your willful failure to perform your duties after Brightfield gives you notice of that failure; or (c) your disclosure of Brightfield's confidential information to anyone who you knew or should have known was a competitor of Brightfield.

2. "Good Reason" means a material reduction in the Base Salary without your consent, and you shall give Brightfield notice of the reduction no later than 30 days after it occurs.

3. If Brightfield ends your employment without Cause, or you end it for Good Reason, Brightfield shall pay you severance equal to 3 months of Base Salary, starting on the 1st regular payroll date that is at least 60 days after the day on which your employment ends.

E. Confidentiality and Prior Agreements

1. You shall not disclose Brightfield's confidential information to anyone, during or after your employment, except as your duties require.

2. You represent and warrant that no agreement prohibits you from working for Brightfield.

F. General

1. North Carolina law governs this letter agreement.

2. This letter agreement is the entire agreement between you and Brightfield on its subject, and no one may change it except in a writing that both you and Brightfield sign.

To accept this offer, you must sign this letter agreement and return it to me no later than January 25, 2027.
