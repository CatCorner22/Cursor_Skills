GUARANTY

This guaranty is dated July 9, 2027, and is between Tillman Brook Holdings, Inc., a Texas corporation ("Guarantor"), and Granite Shoals Capital, LLC, a Texas limited liability company ("Lender").

Background

Lender is providing a revolving line of credit of up to $2,400,000 to Tillman Brook Logistics, LLC, a Texas limited liability company and a subsidiary of Guarantor ("Borrower"), under a loan agreement dated July 9, 2027, between Lender and Borrower (the "Loan Agreement").  Lender requires this guaranty as a condition to making advances under the Loan Agreement.  Borrower is not a party to this guaranty.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

A. Guaranty

1. Guarantor hereby guarantees to Lender the payment of all amounts that Borrower owes Lender under the Loan Agreement (the "Guaranteed Obligations").  This guaranty is a guaranty of payment, not of collection.

2. This guaranty is irrevocable.

3. Guarantor shall pay Lender any Guaranteed Obligation no later than 5 days after Lender demands payment, if Borrower shall have failed to pay that Guaranteed Obligation when due.  Guarantor shall make each payment by wire transfer to the account into which Borrower makes its payments under the Loan Agreement.

4. Lender is not required to proceed against Borrower or any collateral before demanding payment from Guarantor.  Guarantor is liable for the Guaranteed Obligations whether or not Lender has taken any action against Borrower.

B. Waivers

1. Guarantor hereby waives notice of acceptance of this guaranty and notice of any default by Borrower, and no delay by Lender in exercising any right under this guaranty shall operate as a waiver of that right.

2. Guarantor hereby waives its rights under Rule 31 of the Texas Rules of Civil Procedure, Section 17.001 of the Texas Civil Practice and Remedies Code, and Chapter 43 of the Texas Civil Practice and Remedies Code.

C. Reporting

1. Borrower shall deliver to Lender, no later than 30 days after the end of each calendar quarter, Borrower's balance sheet and income statement for that quarter.

2. Guarantor shall notify Lender no later than 10 days after any material change in Guarantor's financial condition.

D. Revocation as to Future Advances

1. Guarantor may revoke this guaranty as to any advance that Lender makes after Lender receives Guarantor's notice of revocation.

2. Revocation does not affect Guarantor's liability for advances that Lender made before receiving the notice of revocation.

E. General

1. Texas law governs this guaranty.

2. Guarantor may not assign this guaranty without Lender's consent.

3. Lender may assign this guaranty to any person to whom Lender assigns the Loan Agreement.
