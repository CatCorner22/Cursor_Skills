## Lint after the run-2 fixes

245 of 303 planted problems found by a direct finding (81%); 184 of 185 lint-detectable; 1 of 117 traps flagged, and that flag is a correct party-article finding next to the trap.

These numbers use a stricter scorer than earlier runs. A code review of the pull request found that the scorer credited any direct finding within 25 characters of a planted problem, even a finding about something else. The scorer now credits a mapped type only when a check that answers that type fires, and any other type only when a direct finding overlaps the problem's own words. Under the old scorer the same lint scored 249; under the new one it first scored 242. 3 of the 7 lost problems were real gaps, now fixed: a reduced passive with no article (*travel expenses incurred by the Consultant*), *shall accrue* (policy, not obligation), and *the same* as a pronoun (MSCD 13.760). The same review found that *shall terminate*, *shall continue* and similar verbs were flagged as policy even when a party had a duty (*Acme shall terminate the subcontract*); the lint now flags them only when the verb has no object.
