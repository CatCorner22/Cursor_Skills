# Riders and proposals: changes to MSCD5 since 2023

Adams updates MSCD between editions on his blog (adamsdrafting.com). A **rider** is a post in which he expressly changes or adds to an MSCD5 recommendation; apply it over the paragraph it names. A **proposal** is a change he only floats; note it, don't apply it. Each rider and proposal is also a record in `data/rules.jsonl` (status `rider` or `proposal`). A check on November 5, 2026, matched every rider below to its post; key quotations are verbatim.

**Swept through:** October 22, 2026 (the newest post found). See "Refreshing the knowledge" in SKILL.md to update.

## Riders (adopted changes to MSCD5)

| Rider | MSCD5 affected | What changes | Post date | Post |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Absence of prohibition becomes an acknowledgment | 3.304 | Write *The parties acknowledge that nothing in this agreement prohibits Acme from selling the Widgets*, not *Acme is not prohibited from* | November 19, 2025 | ["Is Not Prohibited From": I've Changed My Mind!](https://www.adamsdrafting.com/acme-is-not-prohibited-from-selling-the-widgets-ive-changed-my-mind/) |
| *Once* and *when* don't introduce a condition | 3.352-3.385 | Use *if*; delete *once* where an adjective phrase remains. Both words "suggest an expectation that the circumstance in question will come to pass" | not shown | [Replacing "Once" in Contracts](https://www.adamsdrafting.com/replacing-once-in-contracts-a-example-of-sweating-the-small-stuff/) |
| No ampersands | none (new) | Use an ampersand only in a business or law-firm name; in a heading, use a semicolon | not shown | [No Ampersands in Contracts!](https://www.adamsdrafting.com/no-ampersands-in-contracts/) |
| New part-versus-whole variant: *each* or *in the aggregate* | ch. 11 | When the contract attributes a quantity to several items or payments, say *each* or *in the aggregate* | not shown | [A New Variant of Ambiguity of the Part Versus the Whole](https://www.adamsdrafting.com/a-new-variant-of-ambiguity-of-the-part-versus-the-whole/) |
| Right-node-raising pivot ambiguity | 12.75-12.87 | In right node raising, offsetting commas "aren't optional"; Adams renames MSCD's "delimiting commas in coordination" "offsetting commas in right node raising" | August 2026 (listings differ: August 9 or 15); renaming post September 27, 2026 | [A New Kind of Syntactic Ambiguity](https://www.adamsdrafting.com/a-new-kind-of-syntactic-ambiguity/); [Revisiting Offsetting Commas in Right Node Raising](https://www.adamsdrafting.com/revisiting-offsetting-commas-in-right-node-raising/) |
| Ambiguity of the specific versus the general | 7.5; 13.359-13.407 | Structure only: *ejusdem generis* and *including* become 1 named kind of ambiguity, with its own chapter in the 6th edition; no rule changes | not shown | [Say Hi to "Ambiguity of the Specific Versus the General"](https://www.adamsdrafting.com/say-hi-to-ambiguity-of-the-specific-versus-the-general/) |
| Lowercase use of a party's common-noun defined term | 6.10-6.11 | A party's common-noun defined term (*Company*, *Lender*) may also appear in lowercase in its ordinary sense. Adams writes "I now propose an exception" but states it as his rule and asks for no views, so the skill applies it as a rider (open question for Blake) | not shown on the post; the blog index dates it July 5, 2026 | [An Exception to the Rule Against Also Stating Without Initial Capitals](https://www.adamsdrafting.com/an-exception-to-the-rule-against-also-stating-without-initial-capitals/) |
| 2nd-printing corrections | 11.106 and 13 others | 11.106 now ties the *the whole* / *the entire* exception to a single item; the rest are errata (listed in the post, and in the MSCD Project's knowledge file `mscd5-official-updates-and-articles.md`, which this skill doesn't include) | spring 2024 printing | [Changes Made to the 2d Printing](https://www.adamsdrafting.com/changes-made-to-the-2d-printing-of-the-5th-edition-of-mscd/) |

An elaboration, not a rider: Adams's "revised take" on *date* and *day* ([Revisiting "Date" and "Day"](https://www.adamsdrafting.com/revisiting-date-and-day/), July 22, 2026) restates 10.16: *date* is the calendar point and *day* the unit, but insisting on either gains nothing, so leave both alone unless 1 causes confusion.

A related elaboration that works like a rider: state an ability or requirement that exists apart from the contract as a fact, *Acme states that it may sell the Widgets* ([Whether Discretion and Obligation Arise Under the Contract or Reflect the World at Large](https://www.adamsdrafting.com/whether-discretion-and-obligation-arise-under-the-contract-or-reflect-the-world-at-large/), November 2025).

## Adams on AI and MSCD (design guidance for the skill)

- Adams doubts that technology can "digest MSCD and present you with a suitably reworked draft." He suggests instead using technology "to offer explanatory annotations," leaving it to the user to decide what fixes to apply ([Could You Use Artificial Intelligence to Check Whether a Contract Complies with MSCD?](https://www.adamsdrafting.com/could-you-use-ai-to-check-whether-a-contract-complies-with-mscd/), mid-2023; quotations verified).
- For creating contracts he prefers document assembly to generative AI, which "might play useful ancillary roles" ([For Creating Contracts, Document Assembly Is the Clear Winner Over Generative AI](https://www.adamsdrafting.com/for-creating-contracts-document-assembly-is-the-clear-winner-over-generative-ai/); reader-extracted).
