# Changelog: round-10 code fixes (scripts and phrase-variants)

Scope: `scripts/*.py` and `data/phrase-variants.json` in this copy. Backups of each file before each edit sit in `r4/review-final/scratch-code/before/`. Every change has a self-test case; the self-test grew from 2,278 to 2,336 cases.

## Accuracy findings (findings-accuracy.md)

- ACC-001: `NUMDATE` requires a month and day of 1-31 and a 2- or 4-digit year, so `24/7/365` is no date. Cases: `24/7/365` clear; `1/15/2027` and `2/3/2027` flagged.
- ACC-002: `_NEG_MONEY` reads a credit only when the whole cell is in parentheses or carries a leading minus; `30% ($30,000)` restates an amount. Cases: the milestone table and its mirror clear; `($200)` and `-$200` still credits.
- ACC-003: the `category-shall` message for a thing or event subject now applies the has-a-duty test and offers *must* (MSCD 3.154) beside the present tense and *will*; rule ids `R-3.98-shall-future-time` (first, so the severity stays 3) and `R-3.154-must-for-others`. Case: *Final Completion shall occur* message says *write must*, cites both rules, severity 3.
- ACC-004: `INTRO_MIN_WORDS = 6`, `intro_like()` (between/among plus a defined party within the first 60 substantive paragraphs) and `intro_candidates()` feed `party_terms`, its no-between fallback, `document_shape` and `party_collective`. Case: a 10-word intro gives `category-will` and `category-must`, and *the Company* (a nonparty) draws no `party-article`.
- ACC-005: `OPENERS` and `OPENING_PHRASE` (an adverb, an enumerator, 2-word openers such as *prior to*, *subject to*, *with respect to*) replace the 24-word inline list in `serial_lists`. Cases: *Prior to the Closing, Seller and Buyer* and *Immediately after the Closing, Buyer and Seller* clear; *the Units, Parts and Tools* flagged.
- ACC-006: `EMBEDDED_QUESTION` clears `restrictive-which` after a verb of saying or choosing. Cases: *specify which option* and *notify Seller which Units* clear; *any claim which arises* flagged.
- ACC-007: the "The X is $A" conflict pattern requires the amount to end the statement (a period, comma, *payable*, and so on), and never matches `$5,000` inside `$5,000.50`. Case: 2 Lease-Year rents and a per-month fee clear; `The Fee is $500.  The Fee is $600.` still conflicts.
- ACC-008: `nonparty-obligation` on a capitalized subject with no introductory clause read is confidence `low`, and its message says the parties are unknown; with parties known it stays `medium`. Case: *The Trustee shall* as a fragment and with an intro.
- ACC-010: `document_shape` finds the lead-in or the 1st article anywhere before the attachments (not the first 60 paragraphs); an attached form's own lead-in no longer counts. `LEAD_IN_WINDOW` became `INTRO_WINDOW` (used by `intro_like`). Case: a 70-line table of contents before the intro: the recital's *will* is not a duty, the body's is.
- ACC-009: `PROVISO` skips *provided that* after has/have/had/not/been/having, and drops its redundant 2nd alternative. Cases: *has provided that information* clear; *, provided that Seller consents* flagged.

## Robustness findings (findings-robustness.md)

The line-end dehyphenation heuristic is NOT implemented (deferred). Cases: a .txt with a soft hyphen and a zero-width space in a term (no 2nd form, no unused term, note present); a .docx run with a soft hyphen.
- ROB-002: `ATT_LABEL` and `ATT_TAIL` give `EXHIBIT_HEAD`, `SCHED_HEAD` and `EXH_HEAD_ONLY` 1 label pattern: `EXHIBIT "A"`, `SCHEDULE 1.1`, `Schedule 3.2(a)`, `EXHIBIT A FORM OF NOTE`. The reviewer's `,` tail was left out: *Schedule 1, as updated from time to time, lists* must stay body text. Cases: the quoted exhibit's definitions are not defined twice; the 4 heading shapes match; the 2 running-text forms don't.
- ROB-003: `PER_PERIOD` skips a table whose row label, cell or Total label names a rate. Existing round-8 case *Markdown table without outer pipes* had a `Monthly` row: relabeled `Support` (its purpose is the pipe parser; the rate-row table is now, by design, no sum). Cases: the implementation-plus-monthly table, and a `$1,000 per month` cell, clear.
- ROB-004: `main()` writes `--json` before the report goes to stdout. Case: `| head -1` still leaves a complete lint.json.
- ROB-006: `_XML = _xml_parser()` (`resolve_entities=False, no_network=True`) at the 5 `etree.fromstring` sites. Case: an internal entity in a comments part is never expanded (lxml refuses the part; it is noted as unreadable).
- ROB-007: `_rules_data.load_overrides` validates `additions` and the `lint` block's types; `compile_hint()` rejects a hint that matches whitespace alone; `rule_checks` guards the token test. Cases: 3 malformed overrides files rejected, a good one read; `\s*` and `(` rejected, `\bshall\b` accepted.
- ROB-008: `build_references.py` raises `InputError` on a missing `ref_headers/<name>.md` and writes each file through the new atomic `_cli.write_text` (shared `_write_atomic` with `write_json`). Cases: the missing-header error (run against a temporary folder, never `references/`); `write_text` replaces the file whole and leaves no `.tmp-` file.
- ROB-009: `typed_header_row` separates words inside a column with a literal space, so a tab is always a column boundary (no exponential backtracking). Covered by the existing typed-table case.
- ROB-010: `COMMENTS_SHOWN = 10` and a trailing "... and N more comments" line. Case: 12 comments show the line; 10 don't.
- ROB-005: SKIPPED (deferred: the windowed prefix rewrite).

## Probe (probe/report.md)

- Escrow agent: `signatory_roles()` reads *, as escrow agent* (after an entity description) in the introductory clause and adds the role as written and in title case to `PARTY_COLLECTIVE` (and to `EXHIBIT_PARTIES` for an attached form), not to `PARTY_TERMS`: a role label keeps its article under house rule 11, and `PARTY_TERMS` drives `party-article`. Case: the probe's intro with *The escrow agent shall* (unquoted) and with *("Escrow Agent")* both clear `nonparty-obligation` and `party-article`; without the signatory the same sentence still fires.
- Is entitled to: `ENTITLED_DUTY` (a verb of receipt or performance, a payment noun, or *from <Party>*) decides whether `is-entitled-to` is a direct finding; otherwise it is emitted with `source='rules'` (a trigger; the rules channel's own R-3.169 hit is then deduped). Cases: *entitled to receive the Fee* and *to reimbursement from Company* direct; *the entire condemnation award* and *quiet enjoyment* trigger only.
- Quotation: `in_quotation()` now measures the whole quotation (opening mark to the closing mark in the same paragraph) and accepts 3 or more words; applied to `subject_modal_checks` (category-shall, nonparty-obligation, lowercase subjects), `SHALL_CAUSE_UNCONTROLLED`, `NONPARTY_SHALL`, the party *will* loop and the present-tense policy check (category-will), and the *execute* check. Cases: the quoted Required Clause clears and its unquoted form fires; the 4-word Section 1542 quotation clears *executing* while a 2-word quotation does not; a quoted *will* and *shall cause* clear.
- Typed label: `SUBJ_LABEL` strips `Section 3.1(a)`, `Section 3`, `3.1(a)`, `(a)(i)`, `Article II` before the subject. Cases: 4 label shapes give no `category-shall`.
- Reduced passive: `HEADING_PARTICIPLE` plus *the section titled* after the match clears the house form. Cases: *stated in the section titled "Payment."* clear; *stated in the Payment Schedule* flagged.
- `def-shall-verbs` (data/phrase-variants.json): `shall mean` and `shall have the (same) meaning(s)` added; 2 examples added. `_run` drops the inventory's duplicate `shall-in-definition` where the sentence-level finding covers the same span. Case: *"Business Day" shall mean* is 1 finding without and with `--inventory`.
- Percentage: `PERCENT_PAY` and the severity-3 check `percentage-amount-equal-to` (rule `R-14.22-simple-formula`, the record that cites 14.24; no `R-14.24` exists). Cases: *pay Consultant 2% of* flagged; *a fee equal to 2% of* and *an amount equal to 2% of* clear; severity 3.
- Shall cause: `SHALL_CAUSE_UNCONTROLLED` adds landlord, lessor, lessee, licensor, borrower, counterparty, bank, client, governmental authority, and the `NONPARTY_HEADS` roles (escrow and other agents, arbitrators, mediators, accountants, appraisers, experts, title company, escrow holder, spouse, court, judge, referee, surveyor, inspector, trustee, administrator, user). Cases: *the landlord of the Premises* and *the bank* flagged; *its Affiliates* and *its Subsidiaries* clear.
- Capitals body: `format_checks` adds the severity-3 direct check `capitals-body` (8 or more words, all letters capitals, not an ARTICLE/EXHIBIT/roman heading; rule `R-16.18-caps-headings-names`); a paragraph with DISCLAIM, WARRANT, MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS, AS IS, LIABILITY, CONSEQUENTIAL or NEGLIGENCE is a rule trigger whose message says MSCD 16.19 prefers bold but a statute-driven form stays. Cases: the probe sentence flagged at severity 3; its sentence-case form, an article heading and a title clear; the disclaimer is source `rules`.
- Section headings: `heading_of()` (replaces `has_heading`) returns the heading; `outline_checks` adds `section-heading-caps` (severity 3; `R-4.21-section-heading-format`) for a heading of 2 or more words, or 1 word of 4 or more letters, in all capitals; article and exhibit lines are outside `secs`. Case: *A. SERVICES.* and *B. FEES AND PAYMENT.* give 1 finding; the title-case form, *Use*, ARTICLE and EXHIBIT lines give none.
- Party term in capitals: `party_term_caps_checks()` (severity 3; `R-2.96-no-emphasis-party-terms`) flags an all-capitals defined term whose autonomous definition names an entity (`ENTITY_NAMED`) or whose integrated definition follows an entity description. Cases: *"ACME" means Acme Widgets, Inc., a Tennessee corporation* and *ACME WIDGETS, INC., a Tennessee corporation ("ACME")* flagged; *"SOW" means a statement of work*, *"LLC"*, *"GAAP"* clear.
- Malformed input: the "No text found" note fires when no paragraph has a word character (whitespace and punctuation only). Case: a curly-quotes-only paragraph.
