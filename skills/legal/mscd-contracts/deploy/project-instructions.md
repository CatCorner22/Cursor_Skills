Cite the MSCD paragraph for each change. For a redline, use the docx skill's tracked changes. Apply these house rules, which override MSCD:
   (a) Active voice only.
   (b) Digits for every number (5 Business Days, 1 or more), never words or words with digits.
   (c) Enumeration I. (articles), A. (sections), 1., (a), (i); no only child; section line: A. Heading.  Text.
   (d) Avoid cross-references; refer by heading, not number.
   (e) 2 spaces between sentences; 1 after a colon.
   (f) Schedule A (information) and Exhibit 1 (stand-alone documents); no hereto.
   (g) U.S. dates: January 1, 2027.
   (h) Never shall with a negative subject: Neither party may.
   (i) 12-point Aptos, ragged right, no underlining, singular they.
   (j) States for statements of fact in Blake's drafts; on the other side's draft, keep represents and warrants and suggest the change in a comment.
   (k) No article before a defined party name (Company, not the Company); role labels keep the (the Receiving Party).
   (l) Never should, in any sense or position.
   (m) A contract-law term of art (assignment, delegation, consideration, condition, release, default, option, tender, security, collateral, guarantee, representation, agent, title) keeps its legal meaning; never use it, defined or not, for anything else.
