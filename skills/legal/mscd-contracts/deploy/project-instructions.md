Cite the MSCD paragraph for each change. For a redline, use the docx skill's tracked changes. Apply these house rules, which override MSCD:
   (a) Active voice only.
   (b) Digits for every number (5 Business Days, 1 or more), never words or words with digits; fraction words (half) and document names (Second Amendment) keep their words.
   (c) Enumeration for the outline: I. (articles), A. (sections), 1., (a), (i); no only child, but an article may hold 1 section; section line: A. Heading.  Text.
   (d) Avoid cross-references; refer by heading, not number: under the section titled "Payment", never as stated in the section titled "Payment".
   (e) 2 spaces between sentences; 1 after a colon.
   (f) Schedule A (information) and Exhibit 1 (stand-alone documents, including any document with its own signature blocks); no hereto.
   (g) U.S. dates: January 1, 2027. A deadline that decides a right states a time of day and zone (no later than 5:00 p.m. Eastern time on June 30, 2027); write the Nth day before an event, never N days before; starting on [date], never from [date].
   (h) Never shall with a negative subject: Neither party may; for a nonparty subject (No Authorized User shall), restructure onto a party: Customer shall use reasonable efforts to prevent any Authorized User from ..., and Customer is liable for any resulting loss; shall cause only for the party's own subsidiary or board, whose acts are its own (MSCD 3.160-3.161).
   (i) 12-point Aptos, ragged right, no underlining, singular they; a clause that law, regulation, case law, or leading practice requires to be conspicuous takes bold 14-point type, never capitals, with an initial line for each party under it.
   (j) States for statements of fact in Blake's drafts, future facts included, and conform every represents and warrants there; on the other side's draft, keep represents and warrants and suggest the change in a comment.
   (k) No article before a defined party name (Company, not the Company); role labels keep the (the Receiving Party).
   (l) Never should, in any sense or position.
   (m) A contract-law term of art (assignment, delegation, consideration, condition, release, default, option, tender, security, collateral, guarantee, representation, agent, title) keeps its legal meaning; never use it, defined or not, for anything else; data-security terms and a security deposit stay.
   (n) In a written consent, drop RESOLVED, that: The Board approves the Merger Agreement.
