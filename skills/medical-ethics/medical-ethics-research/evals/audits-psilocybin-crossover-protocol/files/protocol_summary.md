# LUMEN-2: Protocol Summary and Pilot Results (Sponsor Draft for Investor Briefing)

**Sponsor:** Brightwater Therapeutics (fictional)
**Product:** BW-101 (synthetic psilocybin, 25 mg oral)
**Indication:** Treatment-resistant depression (TRD)

## Pilot (LUMEN-1), completed

- 30 adults with TRD; randomized 1:1 to BW-101 25 mg or an inert placebo capsule (microcrystalline cellulose) in period 1; all participants crossed over to the other treatment in period 2 after a 4-week washout.
- Each dosing session was supported by one therapist, who also conducted the MADRS interviews at baseline and weeks 1, 3, and 6 of each period.
- **Response** was defined as a MADRS reduction of 25% or more from baseline.
- **Period 1 results:** 9 of 15 BW-101 participants responded vs. 3 of 15 placebo participants.
- **Primary endpoint:** MADRS change at the post-dose week (1, 3, or 6) that showed the greatest improvement for each participant.
- Analysis set: participants who completed both periods (n = 24; 6 withdrew, 5 of them from the placebo-first sequence after period 1).
- Adverse events: recorded when participants reported them spontaneously. No serious adverse events were reported.
- **Durability:** the 9 period-1 responders entered an open-label 6-month extension; 8 of 9 remained responders at 6 months.

## Sponsor headline (investor slide)

> "BW-101 TRIPLED the response rate vs. placebo, and 89% of responders stayed well for six months. LUMEN-2 will confirm this paradigm-shifting effect."

## Planned LUMEN-2 (Phase 2b)

- Same crossover design, same washout, same rater arrangement, same responder definition.
- n = 60 (no power calculation provided; "doubling the pilot will be sufficient").
- Participants must taper off antidepressants within 2 weeks before enrollment.
- No assessment of whether participants or raters could guess their assigned treatment.
- Recruitment through the sponsor's patient-advocacy social media channel, which describes BW-101 as "the breakthrough depression cure."
