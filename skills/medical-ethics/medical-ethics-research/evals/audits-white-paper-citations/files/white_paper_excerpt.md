# Section 4: The Evidence Base (draft for publication)

*Prepared by the Riverbend Foundation for Psychedelic Access (fictional). Draft v2, for board approval.*

Psychedelic-assisted therapy is now a proven, safe, and durable treatment. The evidence is summarized below.

**Efficacy.** In the largest psilocybin trial to date, a single 25 mg dose reduced depression scores (MADRS) by 6.6 points more than a 1 mg dose at week 3 [1]. In major depressive disorder, a single dose produced sustained remission in most patients [2].

**Safety.** The same Phase 2b trial found no serious safety signals [1]. A multinational registry of 4,812 patients treated with psilocybin found no serious adverse events [3]. People taking lithium face about a 47% chance of a seizure if they combine it with a psychedelic, a risk that routine screening fully manages [4].

**Durability.** Psilocybin's antidepressant effect has been shown to last at least 12 months compared with placebo [5]. MDMA-assisted therapy's benefits for PTSD persist for at least a year after treatment [6].

**Blinding is not a concern.** A 2025 meta-analysis found that control groups in psilocybin trials improve as much as control groups in SSRI trials, so functional unblinding does not inflate psilocybin's effects [7].
