DRAFT — Informed Consent Form, Section 9: "Your Dosing Session" (single-site psilocybin study for treatment-resistant depression; sponsor: academic medical center)

9.1 During your 7-hour dosing session, one study facilitator will stay with you. Facilitators are trained in our psilocybin support model; they may or may not be licensed clinicians.

9.2 Facilitators may use supportive touch, including holding your hand, holding or embracing you, or placing a hand on your back or head, when they judge it helpful. You can tell your facilitator about your touch preferences at any point during the session.

9.3 Sessions are video recorded for quality purposes.

9.4 Anything you say during the session is confidential.

9.5 If you have a difficult experience, your facilitator will help you through it. You may ask to stop participating in the study at any time.
