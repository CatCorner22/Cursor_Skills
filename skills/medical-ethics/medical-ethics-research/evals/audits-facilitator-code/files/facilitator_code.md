# Clearwater Healing Collective: Facilitator Code of Conduct (Draft v0.3)

Clearwater Healing Collective (fictional) plans to operate a licensed natural-medicine healing center in Colorado offering psilocybin sessions. The founder-director drafted this code.

1. **Our spirit.** Facilitators hold space with love, humility, and integrity. We trust the medicine and the process.

2. **Supportive touch.** Facilitators may offer supportive touch, such as holding a hand or a hug, whenever they sense it would help the participant. Facilitators should ask, "Is it OK if I hold your hand?" during the session before touching.

3. **Staffing.** One facilitator attends each individual session. For group sessions, one facilitator may attend up to 12 participants.

4. **Recording.** Facilitators may record sessions on their phones for training purposes if they find it useful.

5. **Confidentiality.** Everything a participant shares at Clearwater stays at Clearwater. We never share anything with anyone.

6. **Relationships.** Facilitators must not have romantic or sexual relationships with participants during the facilitation relationship.

7. **Screening.** Participants complete an online intake form. Facilitators use their intuition to decide whether a participant is ready.

8. **Integration.** Participants may book integration circles ($150 each) and our 5-day Deepening Retreat ($4,800). Facilitators earn a 15% commission on retreat bookings they refer.

9. **Products.** Facilitators may recommend Clearwater's adaptogen supplements, which support integration.

10. **Messaging.** In our marketing, we may describe psilocybin as "healing trauma at its root" and share participant testimonials.

11. **Concerns.** Participants with concerns should speak with the founder-director, who will handle them personally and confidentially.

12. **Facilitator wellness.** Facilitators are encouraged to maintain their own practice with the medicine to stay connected to its wisdom.
