LUMORA'S LMR-7 PSILOCYBIN THERAPY CUTS DEPRESSION RELAPSE RISK IN HALF

Phase 2 trial shows breakthrough durability. In the randomized, placebo-controlled LUMEN-2 study, a single 25 mg dose of LMR-7 with psychological support reduced the risk of depressive relapse by 50% over 12 weeks compared with placebo (relapse: 6 of 60 patients on LMR-7 vs 12 of 60 on placebo).

"This is an unprecedented, paradigm-shifting result," said the CEO. 64% of LMR-7 patients achieved a response, defined as a 25% or greater reduction in MADRS score, at week 6. In the 12-month open-label extension, 9 of 10 responders who continued remained well.

LMR-7 was well tolerated. Only 30% of patients experienced headache, which was mild and resolved within 48 hours (placebo: 10%). FDA has granted LMR-7 Breakthrough Therapy designation.
