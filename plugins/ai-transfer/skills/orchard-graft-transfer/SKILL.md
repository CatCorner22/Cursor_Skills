**#33** · **Domain:** Horticulture · **Category:** extension · **Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium

## Core principle

The rootstock stays alive; the scion adds fruit — the graft union is visible.

## AI problem addressed

## Implementation

1. Generate or keep rootstock (safe, general)
2. Generate scion (specialist, bounded)
3. Join at an explicit graft marker; do not replace the rootstock
